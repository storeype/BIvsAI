Empty container repositioning

CMA CGM Europe (synthetic depots) · Over the next four weeks, which European depots receive repositioned empties first, and which depots supply them?

SYNTHETIC DATA. Company-level values on this page are generated by a seeded script and anchored to public data (see the workbook’s Generation_Log and Source_Key). They illustrate the decision rule; they are not facts about the company.

Decision-maker

Equipment-control manager at a container line; weekly cycle.

Data

Real anchor: Eurostat port TEU and empty TEU, 2024, 14 EU ports. Generated: depot stock, 4-week demand and returns (tilted by the real empty share), costs, contribution, forecast error.

Method

Projected balance per depot; depots above safety stock become sources; the rest are scored on shortage, revenue at risk, cost, days of cover, forecast error.

Result

Genova is the one clear deficit (about 9,500 TEU short), first under every weighting; eight depots are sources. On paper the surplus (about 28,900 TEU) covers the shortage (21,300).

Data confidence Low   Result confidence Low

Where the real data lives

Carrier equipment-control systems and container marketplaces (Avantida, xChange): daily stock by depot, bookings, off-hire returns, leasing costs.

Dashboard

Dashboard placeholder: with real data, this decision is better served by a BI tool

Depot stock and bookings change daily, are confidential, and are worked by regional controllers with finance oversight. BI on the carrier’s equipment data; the AI page prototypes the rule.

A demo page on the synthetic data is easy to build, which is why it is left out: it would show the rule, not the decision a company would actually run.
