BI vs AI › Brief 14

Chokepoint exposure and rerouting

Maersk lanes · When to reroute a lane away from a maritime chokepoint, and when to hold the route and absorb the delay.

Decision-maker

Network planning manager at a carrier or forwarder; reviewed twice a week.

Data

IMF PortWatch daily chokepoint transits 2019–2026 (28 chokepoints). War-risk premiums, tolls, fuel and EUA prices from cited sources.

Method

Five signals (traffic recovery, momentum, net reroute cost, detour days, security) → Reroute Signal Index; ≥60 reroute, ≤40 hold; weekly back-test since 2019.

Result

Hormuz lane: REROUTE (97). Red Sea lanes: WATCH (48–58). Panama: HOLD. Back-test: the Red Sea signal fired on 31 Dec 2023 with no false alarms; Hormuz lagged two weeks and gave 18 false-alarm weeks.

Data confidence High   Result confidence Moderate

Dashboard

Dashboard placeholder: this decision is better served by a BI tool

The value of this dashboard is that it refreshes from PortWatch twice a week, alerts the planning desk when a lane crosses a band, and keeps a record of what it showed when each call was made. A static page cannot do that, so the live version is left for a BI platform (or a scheduled data pipeline).

Requirements for the BI build: scheduled PortWatch refresh (weekly at minimum) · band-crossing alerts · role-based access for internal cost inputs · decision log.
