BI vs AI › Brief 6

Choosing the next export markets

Perfetti Van Melle (HS 1704) · Which two non-EU countries the Breda plant’s sugar-confectionery exports should target next year.

Decision-maker

Export manager at a European confectionery manufacturer with capacity for two new markets.

Data

UN Comtrade (public API): HS 1704 imports by country 2019–2025. World Bank GDP per capita and population. LPI 2.0 import dwell. EU trade agreements (team-compiled flag).

Method

63 non-EU markets (≥ US$20m imports) scored on size, growth, income, access and logistics.

Result

UK and Canada, then Norway and Switzerland. The US is the largest market but ranks 8th (no EU trade agreement). PVM likely already sells in all ten: public data cannot tell which markets are new.

Data confidence High   Result confidence Moderate–low
