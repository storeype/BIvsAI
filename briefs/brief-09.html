Air or sea by product line

HEMA (synthetic product lines) · For each product line imported from Asia this season: sea, air, or split (sea base with an air top-up)?

SYNTHETIC DATA. Company-level values on this page are generated by a seeded script and anchored to public data (see the workbook’s Generation_Log and Source_Key). They illustrate the decision rule; they are not facts about the company.

Decision-maker

Supply manager at a European variety retailer; each season or when rates jump.

Data

Real anchor: Comext 2025 EUR/kg by HS chapter; TAC air rate (USD 4.26/kg, Oct 2026); Drewry WCI sea rate (USD 3,504/FEU, 12 Nov 2026); LPI 2.0 transit. Generated: 20 lines’ value, volume, margin, selling window, stock-out cost.

Method

Net air premium per kg after inventory-carrying savings; five criteria; AIR ≥ 60, SEA ≤ 40.

Result

3 AIR (watches, a short-season apparel line, a health-device line), 9 SPLIT, 8 SEA. Watches first in four of five weightings. The split follows mainly from real value densities and rates.

Data confidence Moderate–low   Result confidence Moderate–low

Where the real data lives

Forwarder bookings and contract rate cards (Kuehne+Nagel, DSV); the retailer’s ERP sell-through and markdown history; actual chargeable weights.

Dashboard

Chart: Scatter. Air or sea is decided mostly by value density and how fast a line loses value. Plotting those two shows the AIR / SPLIT / SEA zones; a bar of the index hid why a line flies.
