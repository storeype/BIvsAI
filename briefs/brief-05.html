BI vs AI › Brief 5

Sourcing country shift under tariff change

Decathlon (bicycles & e-bikes) · Whether to move sourcing of a product category to another country when duties or trade-defence measures change, and to which country.

Decision-maker

Category procurement lead at a European sports retailer (Decathlon: suppliers in 45 countries).

Data

Eurostat Comext DS-045409: EU-27 imports by partner, 2019–2025 (pulled through the Eurostat API). EU anti-dumping regulations. World Bank LPI 2.0 export dwell.

Method

12 candidate countries scored on expected duty, proven scale, momentum, unit value and export logistics; countries under €5m of imports not ranked.

Result

Bicycles → Bangladesh (No. 1 under every weighting), then Viet Nam and Tunisia. E-bikes → Sri Lanka and Cambodia. China ranks last for bicycles (62.5% duty).

Data confidence High   Result confidence Moderate

Dashboard

Chart: Scatter. Sourcing trades duty cost against proven supply scale. Duty against EU imports shows the trade-off at a glance (bottom-right is cheap and proven); a ranked bar showed only the result.
