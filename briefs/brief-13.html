Supplier transport emissions

IKEA (synthetic suppliers) · Which six suppliers should the sustainability team engage first to cut inbound transport emissions this year?

SYNTHETIC DATA. Company-level values on this page are generated by a seeded script and anchored to public data (see the workbook’s Generation_Log and Source_Key). They illustrate the decision rule; they are not facts about the company.

Decision-maker

Sustainability/logistics lead at a European furniture retailer; annual programme.

Data

Real anchor: Comext 2025 furniture imports by origin (tonnes, EUR/kg); Eurostat average road haul; truck about 80 g CO2/tkm (Sixfold). Generated: 30 suppliers’ tonnes, distances around the real haul, primary-data flag.

Method

tCO2e from tonne-km by mode; five criteria (emissions, intensity, mode-shift potential, spend leverage, data gap); top six engaged.

Result

Five Chinese suppliers and one Lithuanian (long road lanes); the six cover about half of modelled emissions under every weighting.

Data confidence Moderate–low   Result confidence Moderate–low

Where the real data lives

Supplier shipment records and the retailer’s TMS (weights, lanes, carriers); carrier emission reports under GLEC/ISO 14083; purchase spend by supplier.

Dashboard

Chart: ranked bar (kept). Kept as a bar: the decision is which suppliers come first, and a ranked bar reads as an order.
