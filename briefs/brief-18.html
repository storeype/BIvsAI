Safety stock under lead-time variability

Philips EU DC (synthetic lines) · For each imported product line, raise, hold or cut safety stock at the European DC?

SYNTHETIC DATA. Company-level values on this page are generated by a seeded script and anchored to public data (see the workbook’s Generation_Log and Source_Key). They illustrate the decision rule; they are not facts about the company.

Decision-maker

Inventory planner at a manufacturer importing into a Dutch DC; monthly review.

Data

Real anchor: LPI 2.0 container export dwell by origin and import dwell in the Netherlands (median and IQR). Generated: 15 lines’ demand, variability, cost, margin, current stock.

Method

Safety stock = z·√(LT·σd² + d²·σLT²) with lead-time σ from real dwell IQRs; gaps prioritised on five criteria.

Result

4 lines to raise (about €101k of stock, mostly one line), 1 to cut. The first line stays first in four of five weightings.

Data confidence Moderate–low   Result confidence Moderate–low

Where the real data lives

ERP lead-time history per purchase order (SAP); carrier and forwarder milestones (project44, FourKites); demand history by SKU.
