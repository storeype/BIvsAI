Carbon cost exposure by service

MSC container trades · Which services (trades) carry the full EU ETS surcharge, and which absorb part of the carbon cost.

Decision-maker

Commercial / pricing analyst at a carrier running services into EU ports.

Data

EU MRV 2025 (422 MSC container ships, 8.8 Mt CO2). MSC’s published 2026 ETS surcharge per trade. Hapag-Lloyd and forwarder benchmarks.

Method

Bottom-up ETS cost per TEU per trade, against MSC’s surcharge and the market; four criteria; CHARGE ≥ 60, ABSORB ≤ 35.

Result

7 charge, 9 hold, 6 absorb. Raise on South Africa, India/Middle East, Oceania; review West Africa and Intra North Europe (€60–70/TEU above modelled cost). Highly weight-sensitive.

Data confidence Moderate–high   Result confidence Moderate
