BI vs AI › Brief 15

DC fast-pick zone

Action DC (synthetic pick data) · Ahead of the autumn peak, which product families move into the 90-slot fast-pick zone next to dispatch?

SYNTHETIC DATA. Company-level values on this page are generated by a seeded script and anchored to public data (see the workbook’s Generation_Log and Source_Key). They illustrate the decision rule; they are not facts about the company.

Decision-maker

DC operations manager at a non-food discounter; re-slotting each season.

Data

Real anchor: Comext monthly imports 2022–2025 → seasonal index by group (toys peak 1.36 in September). Generated: picks, cube, affinity and slot need for 40 families.

Method

Five criteria (peak picks, cube, stability, affinity, replenishment); families fill the zone in rank order until capacity.

Result

18 families fill 86 of 90 slots and serve 64% of peak picks. No. 1 changes under three of four alternative weightings: the call is sensitive to the (generated) pick data.

Data confidence Low   Result confidence Low

Where the real data lives

The WMS pick log (Manhattan, Blue Yonder, SAP EWM): every order line, location, pick time and replenishment task, plus the item master with dimensions.

Dashboard

Dashboard placeholder: with real data, this decision is better served by a BI tool

Hundreds of thousands of order lines a week, drill-down by zone and shift: BI on WMS data, or the WMS slotting module.

A demo page on the synthetic data is easy to build, which is why it is left out: it would show the rule, not the decision a company would actually run.

Snapshot chart (SYNTHETIC data)

Chart: Heatmap. Slotting balances volume, cube, stability, affinity and refill effort. A heatmap shows which strength put each family in the golden zone.
