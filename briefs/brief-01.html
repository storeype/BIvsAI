BI vs AI › Brief 1

Berth productivity benchmarking

Rotterdam & Antwerp terminals · Which part of the vessel-turnaround gap against comparable ports should next year’s investment budget target: vessel waiting and berth access, quay productivity, or yard and gate dwell?

Decision-maker

Terminal operations director at a large North European container terminal; annual investment cycle.

Data

CPPI 2025 Annex 2 (World Bank & S&P Global): 373 ports with a 2025 score, berth share, calls. World Bank LPI 2.0 national dwell and turnaround. UNCTAD port calls (context).

Method

Three options scored for the target port against 12 North European peers (≥400 calls) on peer position, distance to best, recent trend and controllability; min-max 0–100, weighted.

Result

Rotterdam → quay productivity (robust under all five weightings). Antwerp → berth access, but it flips to quay productivity under three of five weightings.

Data confidence High   Result confidence Moderate

Dashboard

Chart: Heatmap. Three options, four criteria: the decision turns on why one option wins, so every score is shown. A bar of the index hid that Antwerp’s lead rests on two criteria only.

BI vs AI for this decision

AI page is enough

CPPI is annual and public: an AI-built page with a port selector is enough. A BI tool earns its place only when terminal operating data (crane moves, berth waits) is joined daily.
