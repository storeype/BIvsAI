BI vs AI › Brief 11

Which vessels to retrofit first

Grimaldi Group fleet · Which ships get efficiency retrofits in the next capital cycle, and in what order.

Decision-maker

Fleet sustainability / energy-saving manager at a shipowner with a mixed-age fleet.

Data

EU MRV (THETIS) 2024 and 2025: verified CO2, fuel and distance per ship (87 Grimaldi ships). EUA and fuel prices; Grimaldi-reported air-lubrication savings.

Method

Ships scored on annual CO2, ETS cost exposure, intensity gap vs sister ships, design age and trend; budget cut and payback on top.

Result

Ro-pax ferries first: 8 of the top 10, led by Cruise Roma (No. 1 under all five weightings). A €20m cycle covers 10 ships: about 38,500 t CO2 and €11m saved a year.

Data confidence High   Result confidence Moderate–high
