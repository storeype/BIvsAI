How to read this chart

Click a box with a light bar underneath to follow that branch; use the buttons above to add or remove levels. Hover over a box to see where its data comes from. The tooltip colour shows how confident we are in the data:

  • High
  • Moderate
  • Low

Boxes that are a "function of below" take the average colour of the boxes that branch off them (red = 1, yellow = 2, green = 3), rounded to the nearest whole number (halves round up).
